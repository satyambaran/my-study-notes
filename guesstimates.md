One achievement I am proud of is starting and running my cloud kitchen, Foods and Friends, independently. 4. Share one failure or setback you experienced and what you have learnt from it?
Toward the end of college, my academic performance declined and even I failed in few subjects. I also didn't receive any placement offers, which was very discouraging. Around the same time, my mother's health deteriorated and I had to stay in Patna to support my family. That period affected my confidence and finding a job locally was difficult. Instead of of waiting for an opportunities, I chose to create one by starting my own cloud kitchen. Managing it taught me about resilience, self-belief and importance of taking initiative even during the unpleasant phases.
